Why we built this

Many people who live alone worry about needing a hand. Small things, like a ride home, are hard to plan alone.

We make that planning calm and simple. You stay in charge of your plan.

Show details

Many people who live alone have a quiet worry: what happens if I need a hand? A ride home after a procedure, someone to feed the cat, a way for help to get in. These are small things, but they are hard to plan for alone.

We built Just in Case to make that planning calm and simple. It leans on free local services first, asks before every step, and never pressures anyone. You stay in charge of your own plan.

Leya Jacob Kurzhiparambil, Oluwaseyi Omisore, Joan Arches, and Satoshi Nagae

Your privacy

Here is what we save, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we save

By default, nothing. Your plan stays on this page, and it is gone when you close it.

Show details

Your plan and card printouts are made during your visit and deleted when it ends. So is the AI toolkit's own log of the chat.

Only if you choose

"Save my progress" keeps your plan on this device. "Share with family" saves your helper list on our server.

Show details

Saved progress holds your plan, helper table, card details, review dates, the day you started, and your last 100 chat messages. It stays in this browser. A shared list holds each task, date, time, and name, plus the first name you chose to show.

A readiness summary is made only when you ask and say yes. It is not saved, it shows helpers by first name only, and Just in Case never sends it. You choose who sees it.

Who can see it

Only you, unless you share a list. People with your link see first names and tasks only.

Show details

What you type is sent to an AI service so it can reply. That service is OpenRouter, which passes your words to an AI model, and their own privacy rules apply. Please do not type bank details or account numbers.

Anyone who has your share link can open it, so send it only to people you trust. Your private link, which can remove names or stop sharing, is separate.

If you use Speak, your browser may send your voice to its maker to turn it into text. This site also loads a font from Google, so Google can see that your browser visited.

How long we keep it

Your visit ends when you close the page. A shared list is deleted after 30 days.

Show details

Saved progress stays on your device until you delete it. Your light, dark, text size, view, and reading voice choices stay in your browser too.

How to delete it

Close the page to clear your visit. Use the delete buttons on "Make my plan" for the rest.

Show details

Saved progress: tap "Stop saving and delete". It appears once saving is on.

A shared list: tap "Stop sharing and delete", or open your private link and tap "Stop sharing". The list is deleted for everyone, and the link stops working.

A helper can remove their own name from a shared list. Files you download or print are yours, so delete them from your computer. Clearing your browser data also removes saved progress and your choices.

Our promise

We never sell your data. Just in Case never sends, shares, or books anything without your OK.
